# Three decades of classics: player testing

Open **http://127.0.0.1:8610/#progear**. If the preview is closed, double-click **Start Arcade.cmd**. All game execution and controls stay in the browser. No player emulator, extension or companion installation is required.

The arcade now has **51 cabinets**: the original eight, 14 additions completing the 1980s, all 20 approved 1990s games and the final nine 2000s titles. Your positive 1990s testing cleared this last expansion; all games play in the browser.

## Start a game

Search or scroll the cabinet list, power on, and wait for **CABINET ON**. Click the screen, press **5** for a coin and **1** to start. Cabinet-side coin/start buttons work too. Some original editions need more than one credit for a two-player start. Robotron and the Midway 1990s cabinets acknowledge the original board’s factory defaults automatically on a fresh profile. Mortal Kombat II and NBA Jam TE need two coin presses per player to start or continue with their original factory coinage. Marvel vs. Capcom also needs two coins to start; its original screen shows INSERT 1 MORE COIN after the first. Progear also requires two coins per player to start. Defender, Joust, Ghosts’n Goblins and X-Men have longer original startup sequences. Deathsmiles waits about 42 seconds for its original board startup; add coins once CABINET ON appears.

Sharp pixels and CRT scanlines are display choices. Use the cabinet fullscreen button for a larger game. Pause, volume and control remapping are in the game screen's toolbar.

## Local multiplayer

| Slot | Movement | Actions in cabinet order | Coin / start |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Player 1 | Arrow keys | Ctrl / Z / X / C / V / B | 5 / 1 |
| Player 2 | I / J / K / L (up / left / down / right) | A / S / D / F / G / H (2P games); fourth action Q on 4P games | 6 / 2 |
| Player 3 | T / F / G / H | R / Y / U / O | 7 / 3 |
| Player 4 | Numpad 8 / 4 / 2 / 6 | Numpad 0 / decimal / + / − | 8 / 4 |

The default fourth-player keyboard profile works with Num Lock on or off. Gauntlet supports four simultaneous players, Super Sprint three, and Double Dragon, Bubble Bobble, Joust and Final Fight two. The 1990s also have head-to-head fighters and simultaneous co-op. Neo Turf Masters alternates shots. Earlier cabinets often alternate turns. Robotron uses I/J/K/L as the firing stick, including in alternating play. On a shared keyboard, simultaneous keys can be limited by the keyboard's hardware; separate browser gamepads can be assigned in the toolbar.

## Feedback to collect during player testing

- Game and selected edition; browser and device.
- How far you played and which stage, transition, continue, game-over or ending you reached.
- Whether every movement/action responds as expected, including diagonal firing, pedals, gear/turbo and cooperative joining.
- Any missing sound, audio crackle, stutter, stretched/cropped display, stuck input after focus changes or unexpected operator screen.
- A screenshot and a short reproduction sequence when something feels wrong.

Boot, credits, initial play, keyboard controls, audio signal, local assets and the existing eight receive browser checks. This release is for player testing: every ending, late-game scene, physical controller and long-session timing has **not** been verified. Those remain the next fidelity checks across all three decades.

Street Fighter II, Marvel vs. Capcom and 3rd Strike have three punches then three kicks. Mortal Kombat II follows its distinct arcade order: high punch, block, high kick, low punch, low kick. Attack+jump and directional combinations retain the games’ original special moves.

The Simpsons, Turtles in Time, Sunset Riders, X-Men, NBA Jam TE and Shadow over Mystara support four local players. Turtles in Time and Sunset Riders join through Coin + Attack; numeric Start keys and cabinet Start controls are aliases for Attack. On gamepads, press the original Attack button after adding a credit. Shadow over Mystara uses X to use the selected item and C to cycle items. Its fourth P2 action uses Q to avoid sharing P3’s movement key. Fourth-player third/fourth actions use numpad +/−.

Neo Drift Out uses its original digital steering, accelerator and brake. Neo Turf Masters keeps its original shot meter and button sequence. Neo Geo uses the original English MVS arcade BIOS; no console-port edition is substituted. Street Fighter III is the original Japan 990608 NO CD arcade set, which still runs CPS-3 hardware emulation.

Puzzle Bobble preserves its original Japanese mode menu. After adding credits and pressing Start, the upper choice is solo and the lower choice is two-player versus. Press Down to select the lower choice, then Ctrl to confirm; P2 uses A to launch on the right board. Two independent boards and both launch controls were checked.

In Neo Turf Masters, finish the current shot before P2 adds a coin and presses Start. Both players confirm their golfers (Ctrl for P1, A for P2), then choose a course. Both-player entry and golfer/course selection were checked; a complete alternating two-player round remains for player testing.

NBA Jam TE retains its original initials, birthday, team and instructions screens. Confirm each player’s choices with that player’s action button. During a game, late challengers may wait until the next quarter boundary before joining. All four human players were confirmed together after that transition.

On a fresh Shadow over Mystara profile, the original operator EEPROM starts in **4 PLAYERS 4 CHUTES MULTI** mode, allowing the four separate coin/start slots. Existing saved operator settings are preserved. After the first coin, allow the original title transition to settle before pressing Start. Confirm the selected character with that player’s Attack button and allow the original introduction to finish before adding the other players. All four heroes and their separate health HUDs were confirmed together in live first-stage play.


## 2000s controls and original menus

P1 uses arrows for movement and Ctrl / Z / X / C in the action order below. P2 uses I / J / K / L for movement and A / S / D / F for the same button order, plus 6 for Coin and 2 for Start. Puzz Loop 2 uses Left/Right and J/L as independent rotary dials instead of four-direction movement.

| Game | P1 actions in key order | P2 actions | Local play |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Metal Slug 3 | Ctrl fire; Z jump; X grenade | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |
| Progear | Ctrl shot / held gunner mode; Z bomb; X original auto shot | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |
| Puzz Loop 2 | Left/Right turn dial; Ctrl launch | J/L turn dial; A launch | 2-player VS |
| DoDonPachi III | Ctrl shot / held laser; Z bomb; X auto shot | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |
| Demon Front | Ctrl fire / hold to charge pet; Z jump / hold to glide; X pet-shield toggle | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |
| The King of Fighters 2002 | Ctrl light punch; Z light kick; X heavy punch; C heavy kick | A / S / D / F | 2-player fighter |
| Ketsui: Kizuna Jigoku Tachi | Ctrl shot / held lock-on; Z bomb; X auto shot | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |
| Samurai Shodown V Special | Ctrl light slash; Z medium slash; X kick; C meditate / directional evade | A / S / D / F | 2-player fighter |
| Deathsmiles | Ctrl shoot left; Z shoot right; X bomb | A / S / D | 2-player co-op |

Progear needs **5 twice, then 1** for P1; P2 uses **6 twice, then 2**. Start both players during the original pilot selection, then confirm each pilot and gunner with Ctrl/A. Holding the first action enters the original gunner attack; the third button is the original auto-shot input. Continue pricing has not been separately verified.

Puzz Loop 2 preserves the original **Stage / Panic / Power / VS** carousel. Add credits and press Start, turn the dial until **VS** is highlighted at the front, then confirm with Ctrl. Let the original How to Play sequence finish; it takes roughly 20–25 seconds. P1 Left/Right and P2 J/L turn their separate red/blue board launchers; Ctrl and A fire. Keyboard rotation substitutes for the original cabinet dials, and each player's dial and launch action can be remapped in the toolbar.

Metal Slug 3 retains character selection, branching routes and vehicles. Fire uses the original close-range knife, and Jump enters an available Slug. The King of Fighters 2002 keeps three-fighter team selection and its secret order screen: use the displayed A/B/C choices, mapped to P1 Ctrl/Z/X or P2 A/S/D, to set each team's order. Samurai Shodown V Special uses Ctrl+Z for a heavy slash; C meditates and combines with directions for the original evasive moves. Both fighters retain their original arcade move combinations.

Demon Front keeps its original IGS/calendar/legal introduction before accepting credits. Select a hero and pet; hold Fire to charge the pet, hold Jump to glide, and use the third button to toggle the pet's shield. P2 enters with their own Coin/Start and confirms with A. The World V105 original software and PGM BIOS are retained.

DoDonPachi III is the original **World 2002.05.15 master** edition. Choose each player's ship and Element Doll through its original menus. Tap the first button for wide shots, hold for the slower laser, or use the third button for auto shots. Ketsui is the ordinary **2003/01/01 master** version: select a helicopter and hold the first action for lock-on fire and slower movement. Bombs and original scoring systems are preserved.

Deathsmiles is the original **2007/10/09 master** edition. Wait for CABINET ON, add credits for each player, choose characters with their movement keys and confirm with Ctrl/A. Follow the original tutorial, stage and rank choices. Hold either shot button for its stronger attack; hold both shot buttons together for lock-on. X/P2 D casts the limited bomb. Its normal accurate blitter timing model remains enabled; exact slowdown comparisons with a physical PCB remain for player testing.

The nine new titles have browser checks for original menus, genuine two-player play, action presses/releases and sound. Complete campaigns, alternate routes, secret bosses, loops, continue flows, endings, expert timing, physical gamepad models and long sessions remain for player testing. Report the exact title, edition, stage and input sequence when something feels different from the original.
